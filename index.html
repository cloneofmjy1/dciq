    How this is calculated

    Each switch marks a manufactured product component (MPC) as US-made; off means no domestic credit is assumed, and every listed component is assumed present and new. Module points are multiplied by the US-cell module's share of installed MWdc; the imported-cell module adds zero module points. Inverter and tracker or racking choices apply to the whole project. Production points count only with US manufacture and every listed MPC US-made; under the US-wafer election, cells also need exclusively US wafers. The complete IRS Notice 2025-08 table for the installation changes together, including BOS. The threshold comes from the credit and construction start.
